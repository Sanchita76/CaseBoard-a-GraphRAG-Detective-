# Communities found in the case files

## Group 1: Felix Ortega / Marlow Museum
**Members:** Felix Ortega, Marlow Museum, Silas Crane, Tomas Reed, Sentinel Security, Oona Park, Priya Nair

- Park noted that only Tomas Reed and Felix Ortega ever received that code.
- Sentinel Security had given Ortega the code while he worked at the museum.
- Night guard Tomas Reed told Menon that he saw a white van parked behind the museum at midnight.

## Group 2: Dockside Warehouse / Harbor Catering
**Members:** Dockside Warehouse, Harbor Catering, Ravi Menon, Nils Arden, Crane Auctions

- Crane Auctions and Harbor Catering both rent space at Dockside Warehouse.
- Harbor Catering is owned by Nils Arden.
- Arden told Inspector Ravi Menon that he never lent the van to anyone.

## Group 3: Helen Voss / Blue Heron Pearl
**Members:** Helen Voss, Blue Heron Pearl, Marlow Police, Marlow Library

- Helen Voss discovered the empty display case at 7 a.m. and called Marlow Police.
- Helen Voss confirmed that the Blue Heron Pearl was insured for two million dollars.
- Helen Voss donated rare books to the Marlow Library for the book fair.

## Group 4: Harbor Festival / Greta Holm
**Members:** Harbor Festival, Greta Holm, Pavel Dunn, Ines Calder

- Pavel Dunn helps Holm organise the yearly book fair.
- Ines Calder organises the Harbor Festival every summer.
- Pavel Dunn volunteers at the Harbor Festival as a stage manager.
